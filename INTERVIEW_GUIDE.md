---

## 🏛️ Architectural Highlights to Showcase

### 1. Hybrid Web Speech API & Gemini AI Pipeline
- **Talking Point**: *"Speech processing runs entirely client-side using browser-native `SpeechRecognition` and `SpeechSynthesis` APIs. This avoids latency and heavy WebRTC/audio streaming server overhead. Only clean transcript text is sent to our Gemini AI evaluation engine."*
- **Code Reference**: [voiceService.js](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/src/services/voiceService.js) & [useSpeechRecognition.js](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/src/hooks/useSpeechRecognition.js)

### 2. Modern ESM PDF Parser on Node.js 22
- **Talking Point**: *"We eliminated legacy synchronous PDF parsing workarounds by building a dedicated ESM PDF text extraction module (`pdfParser.js`) running natively on Node.js 22. It parses uploaded candidate resumes into structured skills and experience metrics to customize interview difficulty."*
- **Code Reference**: [pdfParser.js](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/backend/src/services/ai/pdfParser.js) & [resumeAnalyzer.js](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/backend/src/services/ai/resumeAnalyzer.js)

### 3. Decoupled Service-Controller Architecture & Analytics Engine
- **Talking Point**: *"The backend strictly separates HTTP routing from business logic. `analyticsService.js` handles trend detection, 6-axis skill radar calculations, weakness detection, practice plan generation, and CSV exports, keeping controllers thin and highly testable."*
- **Code Reference**: [analyticsService.js](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/backend/src/services/analyticsService.js)

### 4. Database Indexing & Connection Pooling
- **Talking Point**: *"We configured MongoDB Mongoose connection pooling (`maxPoolSize: 10`), connection retries, and compound indexes (`{ userId: 1, createdAt: -1 }`) for fast analytics aggregation and low-latency queries under high traffic."*
- **Code Reference**: [db.js](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/backend/src/config/db.js) & [Interview.js](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/backend/src/models/Interview.js)

### 5. Pure SVG Visualizations & React 19 Performance
- **Talking Point**: *"To guarantee zero external charting dependency overhead and full React 19 compatibility, all charts (Skill Radar, Score Timeline, Doughnut, Bar, Activity Heatmap) are built as responsive SVG components with zero bundle bloat."*
- **Code Reference**: [SkillRadarChart.jsx](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/src/components/analytics/SkillRadarChart.jsx) & [ScoreTimelineChart.jsx](file:///c:/Work/VIVEK/AI%20Interview%20Preparation%20Platform/src/components/analytics/ScoreTimelineChart.jsx)

## 🎬 5-Step Live Interview Demo Script

1. **Step 1: Dashboard & Career Readiness Index**
   - Open `/dashboard`. Highlight the SaaS metrics cards, readiness score, and AI Career Coach observations.

2. **Step 2: Resume PDF Upload**
   - Navigate to `/interview-session`. Upload a sample PDF resume. Show how technical skills and experience levels are extracted dynamically.

3. **Step 3: Real-Time Voice Interview Mode**
   - Start a Voice Mock session. Show the browser reading questions aloud (TTS) and capturing live mic speech transcripts (STT).

4. **Step 4: AI Evaluation Report**
   - Submit answers and view itemized STAR scorecards, strengths, and improvement suggestions generated by Gemini.

5. **Step 5: History & CSV Export**
   - Navigate to `/history`. Demonstrate multi-field filtering, quick report preview modal, and click **Export CSV**.
